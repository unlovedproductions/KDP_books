## Description (HTML allowed in KDP; plain version below)

**Stoic Foundations** is a guided workbook in *The Stoic Collection* for people who know Stoic slogans and still cannot run a method on an ordinary incident — a late message, a short meeting, a plan that slips.

It installs judgment, control, and assent as skills you can repeat:

- See the impression before you treat it as a verdict  
- Separate facts from added judgments  
- Sort what is up to you — including your share of mixed scenes  
- Pause before you agree  
- Own the act without prosecuting yourself  
- Test the next act against virtue  
- Review the day  

The loop name **SEE → SORT → OWN → CHOOSE → REVIEW** is this Collection’s publishing method. It is not an ancient slogan. Classical teaching is quoted from public-domain translations and labeled apart from the worksheets.

This is Book 1, the official starting book. You do not need a prior Collection title. It is not a quote anthology, not a blank journal, and not Book 2’s shadow-work / avoidance title.

When the five moves are available without this book in your hand, Book 2 — *Stoic Shadow Work Journal* — aims the same engine at hidden patterns. You are not required to buy it to have finished this one.
