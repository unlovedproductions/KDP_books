## 5. Production rules — do not repeat

### Metadata (title / subtitle / series)

1. Title field = exact cover title only. No extra descriptors, no keyword lists.
2. Do not put generic format words in title **and** subtitle. Banned-by-example on KDP’s own page: **journal, notebook, gifts, books**.
3. If the product is a guided workbook, put format language in **description** and **keywords**, not as a repeated title-field descriptor. KDP’s reviewer said so in `#51158280`.
4. Do not use bestselling / free / other-author / trademark stuffing in title, subtitle, or series name.
5. Title + subtitle < 200 characters (official subtitle rule).
6. Cover type, author, series name must match metadata. Author string on this SKU was “Unloved Productions”; Collection locks use **UnlovedProductions** — pick one and match cover, copyright, and KDP.
7. Reply to Content Review inside the stated window. Missed 5 days = unpublished. Repeat noncompliance can hit the **account**, not just the SKU.
8. After a metadata block, do not assume an in-place edit keeps reviews/rank. Their template said a guidelines-compliant republish may have to be a **new book**.

### Cover / spine

9. Spine text ≥ **0.375" (9.6 mm)** from top **and** bottom cover edges.
10. Spine text ≥ **0.0625" (1.6 mm)** from left and right spine folds.
11. No spine text under 79 pages.
12. Fix in Previewer, re-upload, wait for re-review. Do not publish past a red quality notice.

### What this SKU did **not** teach

- Interior curriculum quality
- Low-content checkbox (still: never check it for Collection workbooks)
- AI disclosure
- ISBN / pricing
