### A.2 Depth policy

Both books stay **one-job deep**. Do not fatten Book 2 with adversity/emotion chapters to “finish the philosophy.” That would fail the clone/overlap gate and steal future domain jobs if you later upgrade.

### A.3 Fits / tradeoff

**Fits:** Solo-dev capital. Architecture Minimum Viable Complete Collection. Bible pillar “progression over volume.”

That pattern proves “2 can be complete.” It does **not** prove 2 covers every life domain. See §5.

### 5.2 Findings

**Finite 4-book, one theme per volume is a proven Stoic series shape.** Ryan Holiday’s Stoic Virtues series is sold as a **4-book** Kindle series and as a complete signed set: *Courage Is Calling*, *Discipline Is Destiny*, *Right Thing, Right Now*, *Wisdom Takes Work*. One cardinal virtue per book. That is the cleanest analog for Pathway **B**’s “stop at a closed count” and for Pathway **C**’s “one domain per volume” — not for our workbook format. [1][2][3]

**Finite 3-book, one pressure per volume is also proven — as a boxed trilogy, not an infinite spine.** *The Obstacle Is the Way*, *Ego Is the Enemy*, and *Stillness Is the Key* are packaged as *The Way, the Enemy, and the Key*. Reviews treat them as an informal trilogy that can be read independently. Analog for “founding + two pressures,” closer to Architecture’s 3-book Recommended Core than to an evergreen catalog. [4][5]

**2-unit complete is also proven — usually as book + journal, or as a year split across two journal volumes, not as “all of Stoicism in two workbooks.”** Holiday lists *The Daily Stoic* and *The Daily Stoic Journal* as a practice pair (366 days). Ex Animo’s *Stoic Virtues Journal* is **Volume I + Volume II**, each ~six months (print lengths listed at 394 and 375 pages); an ebook combines both for 365 days. Analog for Pathway **A**’s “pair is enough to call complete.” Those products deepen by **calendar length**, not by adding new failure-mode books. [6][7][8][9]

**Single-title depth often means a timed program, not a series.** Brittany Polat’s *Journal Like a Stoic* is a **90-day** guided course at **224 pages**, $14.99 trade paperback — one book, three-stage arc (awareness → acceptance → impact). Independent KDP titles also sell 30-day workbooks. Analog for **per-book depth**: a completable program with a clock. Our Bible already specifies 8–12 instructional chapters and 110–160 typical pages; do not chase 224–394 page daily journals unless a Brief changes format. [10][11]

**Evergreen catalogs exist at publisher scale, not as one numbered workbook spine.** Holiday’s own site frames **12** books plus courses; the Stoic work sits in multiple sub-series (early trilogy, Daily Stoic pair, Virtues quartet). Quarto’s *Everyday Stoicism Journal* sits inside a **theme catalog** of unrelated guided workbooks (productivity, tarot, CBT, etc.). Analog for Pathway **C**’s temptation to keep adding SKUs. That is not a reason to lock six Collection titles before Book 3 exists. [6][12]

**KDP “depth” that matters for this series is legal/product, not page padding.** Official KDP help defines **low-content** as minimal/repetitive fill-in interiors (notebooks, planners, diaries/journals, prompt journals, log books). Those titles **cannot create a series**, cannot take a free KDP ISBN, and have no Expanded Distribution. “Medium-content” is **not** an official KDP class; KDP community staff state it is either low-content or it isn’t. Trade/indie usage of “medium-content” means instructional writing plus write-in — which is exactly the Collection spec. Checking low-content would **kill** numbered series enrollment. Depth = original, non-repetitive instruction + unique prompts, not extra blank pages. [13][14]

**Disagreement:** Seller blogs treat “medium-content series of journals” as a growth tactic. Official KDP treats prompt journals as low-content. This Collection must stay on the **not-low-content** side of that line. Do not lengthen the series by shipping repeating daily grids as numbered Books 3–6.

### 5.3 Cross-cutting patterns

Market length clusters at **2 (pair)**, **3 (trilogy)**, **4 (closed virtue set)**, and **open catalog**. Nothing in session sources says a 6-book guided-workbook philosophy series is the category norm. Depth in comps is usually **days-in-program** or **one theme per volume**, not thinner books to hit a count. That supports the Architecture rule: do not add books to hit a number; do not thin B1/B2 to fund B3+.

4. If you pick **B**, treat C as Expansion, not as a broken promise. Virtues and Relationships can remain unnumbered forever.
