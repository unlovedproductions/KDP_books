# MASTER SERIES BIBLE PROMPT
## The Stoic Collection — Permanent Series Design System
### Version 1.1

Operate according to the MASTER AI ROLE PROMPT.

Your task is to create and maintain the MASTER SERIES BIBLE for:

**Publisher:** UnlovedProductions  
**Series:** The Stoic Collection

The Series Bible is the permanent strategic, editorial, educational, visual, experiential, and branding reference for the entire series.

It is NOT a book outline.

It is NOT a manuscript.

It is NOT a book-specific blueprint.

It is the governing system that allows multiple books to feel unmistakably related while remaining individually valuable and distinct.

---

# PURPOSE OF THE SERIES BIBLE

The Series Bible must establish the rules, systems, terminology, standards, and recurring experiences that define The Stoic Collection.

Every future book should use this document as a foundational reference.

The goal is for a reader to recognize that two books belong to The Stoic Collection even if:

- They cover different subjects.
- They have different chapter themes.
- The covers are not visible.
- The reader opens to a random interior page.

At the same time, the books must never feel like identical templates with different titles.

The series should create a recognizable publishing ecosystem.

# READER REFERENCE & RESEARCH INTEGRITY SYSTEM

Establish a permanent Reader Reference & Research Integrity System for The Stoic Collection.

The purpose is to ensure that every finished book provides an appropriate level of transparency regarding the terminology, philosophical concepts, quotations, and research used within the book.

The system should make the collection feel authoritative and trustworthy without making the books unnecessarily academic.

## 1. STOIC TERMINOLOGY & CONCEPTS GUIDE

Create a standardized approach for explaining important Stoic terminology and concepts used in each book.

The guide should:

- Use reader-friendly definitions.
- Explain important philosophical terminology accurately.
- Distinguish classical meanings from modern interpretations where necessary.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Include only terms genuinely relevant to the individual book.
- Use consistent terminology across the series.
- Follow the series' established editorial voice.

Determine whether the collection should use the term:

**Stoic Terminology & Concepts Guide**

or another standardized title.

The final terminology should become a recognizable series convention.

## 2. SOURCES & CITATIONS

Establish a consistent system for documenting the sources used in each book.

The system should address:

- Primary Stoic sources
- Secondary scholarly sources
- Modern research where applicable
- Quotations
- Paraphrases
- Historical claims
- Psychological or behavioral claims
- Translation identification
- Citation numbering
- Bibliographic information
- Source verification

Determine the appropriate citation style for the series.

The system should be rigorous enough to support credibility while remaining accessible to the target reader.

## 3. FURTHER READING

Establish when a Further Reading section should be included.

It should provide readers with legitimate opportunities to explore:

- Stoic philosophy
- Primary Stoic texts
- Reliable translations
- Relevant scholarly works
- Appropriate modern research

Do not include books or sources merely to make the bibliography appear substantial.

## 4. QUOTATION ATTRIBUTION

Establish permanent standards for quotations.

Every direct quotation must have:

- Correct author
- Correct source/work
- Appropriate translation information where applicable
- Verification
- Appropriate attribution

Popular internet quotations must not be treated as authoritative without verification.

Paraphrases must not be presented as direct quotations.

## 5. SOURCE CLASSIFICATION

Establish a standard distinction between:

- Primary Stoic source
- Scholarly secondary source
- Modern interpretation
- Modern psychology/behavioral research
- Publishing-created framework
- Editorial explanation

This classification should prevent readers from confusing modern publishing methodology with ancient Stoic doctrine.

## SERIES-LEVEL CONSISTENCY

Determine which aspects of the Reader Reference & Research Integrity System are permanent and which can adapt by book.

Permanent standards may include:

- Source integrity
- No fabricated quotations
- Clear attribution
- Consistent citation methodology
- Reader-friendly terminology
- Clear distinction between ancient and modern concepts

Adaptable elements may include:

- Number of glossary terms
- Number of sources
- Further-reading depth
- Research-note requirements
- Citation density

## BACK-MATTER PHILOSOPHY

Reference material must contribute to the reader experience.

Do not use citations, glossaries, or bibliographies as artificial page-fillers.

Every reference section must answer a legitimate reader need:

- "What does this mean?"
- "Where did this idea come from?"
- "How can I explore this further?"
- "Which parts come from classical Stoicism?"
- "Which parts are modern interpretations or publishing-created frameworks?"

## READER TRUST STANDARD

The Reader Reference & Research Integrity System should strengthen the reputation of UnlovedProductions and The Stoic Collection as a serious, responsible, high-quality publishing brand.

The collection should be accessible to general readers while maintaining a level of source integrity that distinguishes it from generic inspirational journals.
