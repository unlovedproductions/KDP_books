## SECTION 1 — STRATEGIC FOUNDATION

**Vision:** Install the series method so later volumes can apply it under harder domains.

**Mission:** Train the reader to catch an impression, withhold rash assent, split control (including *your share*), act on what is theirs, and review the day—without turning Stoicism into numbness or quote collecting.

**Core promise:** After this book, the reader can run Control Split, Assent Pause, and Evening Review on an ordinary bad hour, and state the method in their own words on the Method Card.

**Reader transformation (this title):**

| Start | End |
| --- | --- |
| Fused with the first hit; “Stoic” means maxims | Separates impression / judgment / act |
| Treats control as wishing the world quieter | Sorts up to you / not up to you / your share |
| Journals mood or skips practice | Completes a 12-chapter arc with live charges |
| Cannot name what to do next under heat | Holds a filled Method Card |

**USP:** The only volume that *installs* the house method. Not a sampler. Not 365 prompts. Not a history of Stoicism.

**Competitive positioning:** Same as Bible §1.7. This title wins Look Inside by showing a lesson + CONTROL SPLIT or ASSENT PAUSE by page 10 of the body—not lined paper.

**Series positioning:** Required first volume. Volumes 2–5 assume this method or their Method Primer. This book must not steal Adversity, Emotion-under-fire, full virtue curriculum, or relationship ethics.

**Product category:** Medium-content guided workbook. Standard KDP paperback (not low-content notebook). BISAC: verify live — likely SEL031000 Self-Help / Personal Growth / General + PHI002000 Philosophy / History & Surveys / Ancient & Classical (or Stoicism node if available). `[[VERIFY KDP]]`

**Primary differentiator:** Assent training + Control Split as *skills*, taught before any “be tougher” rhetoric.

**Reader value proposition:** A finishable 3–5 week program that leaves a portable operating card.

**Brand contribution:** Teaches the names and visual blocks every later book will reuse. If this volume is sloppy, the series has no brand.

**Reasoning:** Foundations-first is a prereq decision (Bible §4.1). Leading with anxiety/adversity keywords would sell faster and produce a clone journal. Trade-off accepted.

## SECTION 2 — READER ARCHITECTURE

Inherits Bible §2. Volume 1 specifics:

**Primary reader friction:** They “already know” Enchiridion 1 from Instagram. Architecture answer: Chapter 1 opens on *their* incident, not a lecture; Chapter 3 names the popular errors (nihilism, passivity, suppression) and kills them with mixed-case work.

**Secondary:** Gift buyer. Front matter + Ch 1 must look like a real book in Look Inside.

**Objections unique to Vol 1:** “Too basic.” Counter: Band II–III by Ch 6–9; later chapters require earlier written artifacts. “Too abstract.” Counter: every chapter ends in PRACTICE CHARGE with cue/behavior/evidence/window.

**Abandonment spike:** After Ch 3 (doctrine feels familiar) and after Ch 9 (hardest heat). Counters: Ch 4 mixed cases (new), Ch 7 Method Card draft (sunk-cost artifact), Re-entry page, Ch 12 lapse-without-drama.

**Completion motivators:** 12 short chapters; Progress Marks; pretest vs posttest; Method Card; honest next-volume job (Adversity).
