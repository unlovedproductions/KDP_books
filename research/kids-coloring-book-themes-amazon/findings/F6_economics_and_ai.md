# F6 — June 2025 royalty cut + AI dumps make “cheap kids book, hope it ranks” a losing play

**Status:** confirmed  
**Sources:** [17](../sources/17_kdp_print_royalty_change_2025.md), [16](../sources/16_kdp_kindle_content_quality.md), [14](../sources/14_kdp_low_content_books.md), [13](../sources/13_kdp_content_guidelines.md), [19](../sources/19_kdp_community_32_books_no_sales_2025.md), [21](../sources/21_mari_kii_kdp_coloring_launch_failure.md)

Official: as of June 10, 2025, print royalty is 50% below $9.99 / €9.99 / £7.99, 60% at or above. Kids coloring historically sat $5.99–$8.99. Color print costs were cut in some marketplaces the same day — partial offset.

Coloring books are **not** low-content ([14](../sources/14_kdp_low_content_books.md)). They are also **not suited to Kindle eBook** ([16](../sources/16_kdp_kindle_content_quality.md)). Duplicate/rebranded interiors are disappointing content.

AI interiors are allowed if disclosed, and still fail if they look generic, resemble trademarks, or are free-web clip art. One Dec 2025 publisher: 32 books, zero sales. One Jan 2026 first-person case: €40 ads → 4 sales, BSR ~4 million (wrong niche, but the traffic wall is real).
