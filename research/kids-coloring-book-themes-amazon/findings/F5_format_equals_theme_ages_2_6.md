# F5 — For ages 2–6, format is as predictive as theme

**Status:** confirmed as a list-pattern; not a controlled conversion test  
**Sources:** [01](../sources/01_amazon_us_childrens_coloring_bestsellers_3374.md), [02](../sources/02_amazon_us_new_releases_3374.md), [08](../sources/08_amazon_uk_theme_serps.md), [03](../sources/03_amazon_us_childrens_activity_bestsellers_3373.md), [19](../sources/19_kdp_community_32_books_no_sales_2025.md)

Recurring product language on titles that rank: “Simple & Big,” “Jumbo,” “Thick Bold Lines,” “Ages 2–4 / 2–5,” “one image” implied by toddler books, perforated pages, dot markers, water-reveal (brand, not KDP). UK’s highest-review indie paperback in the toddler scrape is Simple & Big Coloring Book for Toddler (7.7K). **Contamination:** adult “Bold and Easy / Cozy Spaces” titled “for Adults and Kids” owns #1–#2 of the *children’s* coloring node. That is Coco Wyo’s all-ages product, not proof that parents of 4-year-olds want kawaii capybaras. Do not chase adult cozy as a kids first title.
