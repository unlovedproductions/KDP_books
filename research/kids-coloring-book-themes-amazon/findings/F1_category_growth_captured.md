# F1 — Kids coloring demand is real; chart share is not open

**Status:** confirmed  
**Sources:** [11](../sources/11_pw_circana_activity_books_2026.md), [12](../sources/12_circana_2026_book_trends.md), [01](../sources/01_amazon_us_childrens_coloring_bestsellers_3374.md), [20](../sources/20_pinterest_parenting_trend_report_2026.md)

Circana via Publishers Weekly (Aug 14, 2026): children’s activity print units 16.7 million in 2025, +18% vs +3% for children’s overall; +18% YTD through May 23, 2026. Circana Q1 2026: screen-free categories including coloring books are growing. Pinterest 2026: parent searches for screen-free activities +200%.

The same PW piece says **a quarter of all US coloring-book units this year are Coco Wyo**. Amazon node 3374 on 2026-08-20 has Cozy Cuties #1 and Cozy Corner #2, both titled “for Adults and Kids.” Licensed Bluey, Mario, KPop Demon Hunters, Melissa & Doug, Paint by Sticker Official occupy other top-20 slots.

**Implication:** “High demand” is true at category level and false as an invitation to upload a generic kids coloring book.
