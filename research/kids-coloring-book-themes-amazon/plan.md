# Investigation plan — Kids coloring book themes that sell on Amazon

**Slug:** `kids-coloring-book-themes-amazon`  
**Date opened:** 2026-08-20  
**Depth:** deep  
**Genre:** decision  
**Question (reframed):** Which 6–10 kids coloring book themes should a solo, unbranded KDP publisher produce first so a new listing can actually move copies on Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, and Amazon.de for ages 2–12?

High BSR on Disney / Bluey / Paw Patrol is demand we cannot legally take. Fast sales for an independent publisher = buy-intent keywords + enough volume + room to rank + no IP landmines + a format parents will click (age on cover, line weight, page count, price).

## Falsifiable hypotheses

- **H1 — Evergreen volume:** Animals, dinosaurs, unicorns/mermaids, vehicles/construction, and farm/pets occupy the highest-demand kids coloring clusters on US/UK/DE bestseller lists in 2025–2026.
- **H2 — Niche ranks faster than generic:** Age-banded + specific titles (`toddler farm animals`, `construction vehicles ages 4-8`, `alphabet ocean`) convert for new publishers faster than `kids coloring book`, because the generic bucket is occupied by licensed brands and mega-catalogs.
- **H3 — Seasonal is velocity, not a first title:** Halloween / Christmas / Easter spike hard in a 4–8 week window and are a weak year-round first SKU unless the next holiday is ≥8 weeks out. (Next major kids gift window after 2026-08-20 is Halloween → Christmas.)
- **H4 — Licensed BSR is a trap:** Character books dominate Children’s coloring BSR. Independent “inspired by” titles are an IP / KDP content-review failure mode, not a shortcut.
- **H5 — Format equals theme for ages 2–6:** Bold/easy thick lines, one large image per page, and an age callout on the cover predict conversion as much as the theme itself.

Each hypothesis is confirmed, refuted, or marked under-determined against ≥3 independent, differently-typed sources. Claims with fewer sources are labeled insufficient evidence.

## Information sourcing strategy

KDP niche blogs (kdpeasy, BookIllustrationAI, LessonCraftStudio, coloringbook.dev) are **industry/affiliate**. They seed candidate themes. They do not decide the ranking.

| Subtopic | Channels | Pull |
|---|---|---|
| Live demand | Amazon primary (browser / fetch) | US/UK/DE bestseller lists; top titles: theme, age, BSR, price, review count, licensed vs indie |
| Keyword buy-intent | Amazon search result pages | Seed queries per age band; sponsored vs organic; licensed share of top 10 |
| Velocity | Keepa/CamelCamelCamel public pages if free; else snapshot BSR + review count as weak proxy | Flag missing history |
| Off-Amazon demand | Google Trends, Pinterest | Theme interest vs Amazon echo chamber |
| Seller reality | forum-discussion (r/KDP, r/selfpublish) | What sold vs guru claims; AI/low-content rejections |
| Legal | regulatory-legal (KDP help) | Licensed characters, duplicate interiors, AI disclosure |
| Trade | industry-specific / news-current | Category growth; gift calendar |
| Opposition | dedicated disconfirming queries | Saturation, no-sales, AI bans, licensed dominance |

**Capability audit (2026-08-20):** No Keepa, Helium10, or Publisher Rocket MCP. No paid keyword APIs. Rank on Amazon snapshots + Trends + forums + official KDP docs. Exact search-volume numbers will be marked unavailable.

### Seed queries

US/UK: dinosaur, unicorn, mermaid, vehicles/trucks/construction, animals, farm, ocean/under the sea, space, alphabet/ABC, Halloween, Christmas, princess (IP-adjacent), toddler coloring, bold and easy kids.

DE: Ausmalbuch + Dinosaurier, Einhorn, Meerjungfrau, Fahrzeuge/Bagger, Tiere, Bauernhof, Meer/Ozean, Weltall, ABC/Alphabet, Halloween, Weihnachten, Prinzessin, Kleinkind.

### Opposition queries (mandatory)

- kids coloring books oversaturated KDP 2026
- Amazon KDP coloring book no sales
- low content coloring books declining Amazon
- KDP AI coloring book rejected
- licensed coloring books dominate Amazon bestsellers

## Risk register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Amazon blocks scrapes / captcha | Browser snapshot; if blocked, use publicly quoted BSR from secondary sources and flag |
| Adult “bold and easy / cozy” contaminates kids demand | Separate adult coloring lists; do not score them as kids |
| KDP-guru blogs recycle each other | Treat as one source type (industry/affiliate); require Amazon + legal + forum triangulation |
| Licensed titles inflate “demand” | Split every top-10 into licensed vs indie |
| Single-day BSR snapshot | State as snapshot dated 2026-08-20; no invented history |
| DE/UK language and holiday calendar differ | Capture all three; do not overfit US |

## Stop criteria

- ≥20 saved sources; each thesis has ≥3 different source types or is marked insufficient
- US + UK + DE bestseller or search snapshots captured
- Licensed vs indie split documented on the top lists
- H1–H5 each judged
- `refresh_targets.md` lists category URLs, seed keywords, and re-check protocol

## Out of scope

Writing or illustrating a book; keyword-stuffed listing copy; adult coloring except as contamination check; TAM/SAM/SOM with invented BookScan numbers.
