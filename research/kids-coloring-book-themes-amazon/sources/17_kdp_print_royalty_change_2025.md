## Verbatim quotes

> Starting on June 10, 2025, we've reduced the royalty rate for print books from 60% to 50% for books priced below: 9.99 USD, 9.99 EUR, 7.99 GBP, 13.99 CAD, 13.99 AUD, 99 SEK, 40 PLN, and 1000 JPY.

> Books with list prices at or above the amounts listed will continue to earn a 60% royalty rate.

> Separately, as part of our annual printing cost review, we reduced color printing costs for paperbacks in some marketplaces

## Notes

Kids coloring historically clustered $5.99–$8.99. That band now earns 50% royalty in US/DE; UK threshold is £7.99. Color print cost cut on .com (standard color) and EU/UK (premium color) is a partial offset, not a restoration of 2019 unit economics.
