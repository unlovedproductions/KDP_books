## Verbatim quotes

> The KDP Terms and Conditions and Content Guidelines require that you hold the publishing rights to any content you upload for sale on Amazon.

> Uploading or attempting to upload any content for which you do not have rights can result in the rejection or removal of your book, negative impact to your account status, and/or loss of royalties.

> A trademark is a word, symbol, or design (such as a stylized brand name or logo) that a company uses to identify its goods or services and to distinguish them from other companies' goods and services.
