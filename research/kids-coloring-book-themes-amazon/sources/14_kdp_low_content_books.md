# 14 — KDP Low-Content Books (official) — coloring books are NOT low-content

- **url:** https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM
- **fetched:** 2026-08-20
- **channel:** regulatory-legal
- **source_type:** official
- **credibility:** 5/5
- **recency:** 5/5
- **bias:** Classification policy. Mis-checking the low-content box can reject a coloring book.

## Verbatim quotes

> This does not typically include activity books, such as puzzle books or coloring books, which generally do not feature repetitive content on each page.

> Coloring books (listed under Not Generally Low-Content)

> Under Categories you need to check the Low-content box. Please note that if you do not select this box, or select a different box, your book will be rejected.

## Notes

Last quoted sentence applies to actual low-content titles, not coloring books. Coloring books should not take the low-content path. Opposition claim 'Amazon killed low-content coloring' is a category error.
