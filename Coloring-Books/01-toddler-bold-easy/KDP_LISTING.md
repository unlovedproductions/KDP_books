## Description (paste)

Big Easy Coloring Book for Toddlers Ages 2-5

100 simple pictures. One big picture on every page. Thick lines for little hands.

Animals, trucks, foods, and everyday things. No licensed characters. Original drawings made for crayons.

- Ages 2-5
- 100 unique easy pictures
- Thick closed lines and lots of space to color
- 8.5 x 11 inches
- Crayons first. If you use markers, slide cardboard behind the page.

A quiet activity book for toddlers and preschoolers at home, in the car, or waiting rooms.
