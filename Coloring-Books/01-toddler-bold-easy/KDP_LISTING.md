## KDP picker paths (US paperback)

KDP lets you pick **3**. The modal shows BISAC-style browse paths, not the old “Children’s Coloring Books” label. Official rule: pick categories that match the book. Do not pick to game rank. ([KDP Categories](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G200652170), [Metadata Guidelines](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201097560))

**Remove** `Children’s Books > Activities, Crafts & Games > Activity Books > General` if it is your only pick, and **do not** save anything under `Children’s Books > Animals > …` (Baby Animals, Cats, Dogs, etc.). Those are picture-book animal nodes. This title is a mixed toddler coloring book, not an animal storybook.

Click, in order:

1. **Required.** `Books > Children's Books > Activities, Crafts & Games > Activity Books > Coloring Books`  
   If `Coloring Books` is not under `Activity Books`, go up one level and take `Activities, Crafts & Games > Coloring Books`.
2. **Second.** `Books > Children's Books > Activities, Crafts & Games > Activity Books` (the `General` leaf you already had is fine as slot 2 once slot 1 is Coloring Books).
3. **Leave empty.** Do not take Animals, Dinosaurs, Trucks-as-story, or adult `Coloring Books for Grown-Ups`. Theme keywords carry trucks/animals, not a false animal-book shelf.
